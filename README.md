# NYC flights 2023: predicting late and cancelled departures

A travel agency that books flights from New York wants to warn clients before a flight leaves late or is cancelled. This project cleans the 2023 NYC flights data, finds what drives delays, groups destination airports, defines the numbers to follow every month, builds two predictions, and turns them into decisions the agency can act on.

**Status: work in progress.** Decisions, results and problems are recorded as they happen in [`second-brain/`](second-brain/).
